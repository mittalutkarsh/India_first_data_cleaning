Feature 4 · implementation

Immutable shards + manifests

Tokenize the cleaned corpus into fixed-size, content-addressed uint16 shards (whole documents, never split), each with a manifest (hash, tokens, lane, tiers, source doc ids) and a shard index; re-hashing proves immutability.
