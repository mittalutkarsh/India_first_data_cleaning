Language Identification

This page does not clean text — it judges it. Each document was filed as hi / Deva; the audit tests whether that is true and decides what to do with it.

How to read this page

Everything here came from a folder labelled "Hindi" — but labels lie. Real corpora hide English, mislabelled languages, code-mixing and junk. Before spending training compute, every document must be checked and routed to one of four outcomes:

ACCEPTReal target-language text → goes into training.
REVIEWEvidence is unclear → hold for a human or a stronger detector.
EXCLUDEAnother language, or no language at all → dropped.
QUARANTINEContent conflicts with its label → set aside and audit the source.

Pick a document below. The 🧪 examples are a guided tour of the situations an auditor hits; the rest are real Hindi Wikipedia. The verdict is decided in two tiers — a dependency-free script check (which alphabet?) plus a fastText model (which language?). Script alone cannot tell Hindi from Sanskrit, nor romanized Hindi from English — which is exactly why both exist. Model tier: fastText verdicts baked in.
