Decontamination

Per decontaminate_SKILL.md: keep the evaluation benchmark honest. Freeze the test items, scan the training corpus for copies of them, and remove the training side — so the benchmark still measures real ability, not memorization.

How to read this page

Each training record is checked against a frozen benchmark (5 items). If a benchmark question — or its answer — leaks into training, that record is routed:

✅ KEEP (clean / source)No item leaks, or only its background passage overlaps.
✂️ REMOVEAn exact / near copy, or a question+answer leak — dropped from training.
🟠 REVIEWA likely translated leak the lexical scan can’t confirm.
🔴 QUARANTINESuspected benchmark-answer poisoning.

Seeing a public source passage is not cheating; a question with its answer is the worst case. The slider selects a threshold bundle (from threshold-calibration.md). The impact panel shows the clean-subset — only uncontaminated items give a trustworthy score. Cross-lingual/semantic matching and real poisoning validation are documented offline tiers.
